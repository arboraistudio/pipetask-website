Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about PipeTask support, data requests, and technical operations.

What information should I include in a bug report?

Include your Chrome version, whether you are using a personal or Google Workspace account, the specific Google Chat Space type, and the exact step where an error occurred. Screenshots of the side panel are always helpful.

How do I request deletion of synchronized data?

Send an email from your authorized Google account to contact@arboraistudio.com with the subject 'PipeTask Data Deletion Request'. Synchronized task records stored in our Supabase workspace will be purged within 30 days.

Does PipeTask access my private chat messages?

No. PipeTask connects strictly to Google Chat task endpoints to synchronize tasks in Spaces you select. It does not read, store, or monitor unrelated message threads or personal chat history.
